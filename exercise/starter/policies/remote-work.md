# Remote Work Policy

## Working from home
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week, agreed with their line manager. Tuesday is the company-wide office day.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and needs approval from the People Team before travel, because of tax and social-security rules.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and one monitor. A home-office allowance of 300 EUR is paid once, in the first month of employment.
