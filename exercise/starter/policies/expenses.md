# Expenses Policy

## Meals on business travel
Meals are reimbursed up to 45 EUR per person per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Receipts
A receipt is required for every expense above 25 EUR. Expenses without a required receipt are not reimbursed.

## Deadline
Submit expenses within 30 days of the date on the receipt. Later claims need approval from the Finance director.

## Travel class
Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Train travel is second class unless the price difference is under 20 EUR.
