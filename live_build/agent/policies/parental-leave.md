# Parental Leave Policy

## Overview
The company offers paid parental leave to all parents, including adoptive parents. Length and pay depend on your contract and local law.

## How to apply
Parental leave is always arranged in a conversation with the People Team, ideally 3 months before the expected date. There is no self-service form.
