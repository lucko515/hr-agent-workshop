# Travel Booking FAQ

## How do I book?
Use the travel portal. Book at least 14 days ahead where possible.

## What is the meal limit?
Meals on business trips are covered up to 60 EUR per person per day.

## Hotels
Hotels are covered up to 140 EUR per night in most cities.
