# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement
Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata share. Public holidays are in addition to this entitlement and are never deducted from it.

## Notice
Requests for 1 to 5 working days must be submitted at least 2 working days before the first day of leave. Requests for more than 5 working days must be submitted at least 10 working days before the first day of leave.

## Maximum length
A single request may cover at most 15 consecutive working days. Longer absences need written approval from a director and are handled by the People Team, not through self-service.

## Approval
Every request goes to the employee's line manager, who approves or declines it within 3 working days. Submitting a request does not approve it.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year and must be used by 31 March. Days beyond that are lost unless the People Team agrees otherwise in writing.

## Year-end closure
The company does not accept annual leave requests for 21 to 31 December from the Operations department, because of year-end closing. Other departments may request these dates as normal.
