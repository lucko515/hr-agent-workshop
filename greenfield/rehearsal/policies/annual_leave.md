# Annual Leave Policy
Version: 1.0
Section: Entitlement
Employees receive 25 working days of annual leave per calendar year.

Section: Requests
The employee confirms the exact dates before the application stores a request.
A stored request remains pending_manager_approval. Only a manager can approve leave.
Working days in this workshop mean Monday through Friday. Public holidays are out of scope.

Section: Unresolved cases
If the employee cannot identify the leave category and asks for HR, record the question
for a human. Do not guess a category or submit an annual leave request.
