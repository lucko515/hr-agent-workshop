# Sick Leave Policy

## Reporting
If you are unwell, tell your line manager by 09:30 on the first day of absence, by phone or message.

## Medical certificate
Absences of more than 3 consecutive working days need a medical certificate, sent directly to the People Team. Do not send medical documents to your manager or to any chat assistant.

## Who handles it
Sick leave is recorded by the People Team. It is never booked through annual leave self-service and is not deducted from annual leave.
