# Onboarding Checklist

## Before day one
The People Team sends your contract, a laptop order form and your start-day schedule.

## Week one
Collect your laptop from IT, complete security training within 5 working days, set up two-factor authentication, and meet your onboarding buddy.

## First month
Complete the data-protection course, agree goals with your manager, and submit your home-office allowance claim.
